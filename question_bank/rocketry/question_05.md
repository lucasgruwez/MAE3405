# Question 05: Kerosene–oxygen rocket with an open gas generator

Source: [Weeks 11–12 problem set](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%2011-12.pdf), Question 33B, PDF page 2 (printed page 16).

A rocket burns a stoichiometric mixture of kerosene, approximated as \(\mathrm{C_{12}H_{26}}\), and oxygen. The reactants enter at −23°C. The main chamber operates at stagnation pressure 7.0 MPa. Kerosene has formation enthalpy −291 MJ/kmol, \(c_p=2\ \mathrm{kJ/(kg\,K)}\) and nominal molecular weight 170 kg/kmol.

The motor uses an open gas-generator cycle. It diverts 5% of the **total** propellant flow to the preburner; the remaining 95% burns in the main chamber. The combustor and preburner each have combustion efficiency 90%.

Assume complete combustion to CO₂ and water vapour, constant species heat capacities and no dissociation. Apply combustion efficiency to chemical heat release. Neglect preburner-exhaust thrust; the stated main-chamber pressure includes the operation of the turbopumps.

At an altitude of 20,000 m, determine:

(a) The thrust produced by a converging main nozzle of exit diameter 0.500 m and isentropic efficiency 95%.

(b) The total propellant flow rate, including the diverted flow.

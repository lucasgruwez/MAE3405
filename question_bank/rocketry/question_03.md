# Question 03: One-stage versus two-stage payload

Source: [Weeks 11–12 problem set](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%2011-12.pdf), Question 31, PDF page 2 (printed page 16).

A rocket launches a deep-space probe. Its total initial mass, including the probe, is limited to 1000 kg. The effective exhaust velocity is 4500 m/s. The design must achieve an ideal velocity increment equal to the Earth's surface escape speed. Neglect gravity and drag losses.

For each stage, the structure mass is 7.5% of that stage's total ignition mass, including everything carried above it. Assume identical stage mass ratios and the same effective exhaust velocity for every stage. Discard each stage's structure after its propellant burns.

Compare designs with:

(i) One stage.

(ii) Two stages.

For each design, calculate the maximum probe payload and total propellant mass.

For the two-stage design, also state the structure and propellant mass of each stage.

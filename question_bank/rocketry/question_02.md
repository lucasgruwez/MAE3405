# Question 02: Sea-level launch thrust and burnout height

Source: [Weeks 11–12 problem set](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%2011-12.pdf), Question 30, PDF page 1 (printed page 15).

A single-stage rocket launches a 100 kg satellite from rest at sea level. The rocket structure has mass 3000 kg. It burns for 75 s at a constant flight-path angle of 85° above horizontal. Its chamber stagnation pressure is 3.0 MPa and temperature is 2750°C. An ideal converging-diverging nozzle has throat area 0.100 m² and expands the exhaust to standard sea-level pressure. The exhaust has specific heat ratio 1.15 and molecular weight 22 kg/kmol.

Assume constant chamber conditions and propellant flow, with the structure and satellite retained until burnout. Neglect aerodynamic drag and Earth curvature, and use constant gravity during the burn. For the trajectory calculation, take the effective exhaust velocity as its launch value and neglect the changing ambient-pressure thrust correction.

Determine:

(i) The launch thrust at standard sea level.

(ii) The vertical motor burnout height.

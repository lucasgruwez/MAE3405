# Question 12 — Dynamometer performance at 2000 RPM

- Source: [Problem set Q10](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20-%20Week%203-4.pdf#page=3), PDF page 3.

A 4-stroke air-cooled 4-cylinder horizontally opposed spark ignition aircraft engine runs on Avgas with $(A/F)_s=14.7$. Its dynamometer torque-arm radius is 0.381 m. Test-cell temperature is 21.1°C and pressure is 101.3 kPa. Cylinder bore is 82.6 mm and stroke is 102 mm.

The measured brake loads are net loads at the stated torque arm; air volume is measured at test-cell conditions.

| Speed (RPM) | Brake load (N) | Air (L/min) | Fuel (L/min) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2000 | 525 | 1905 | 0.210 |
| 2700 | 506 | 2360 | 0.278 |

At **2000 RPM**, calculate:

(i) torque;

(ii) brake power;

(iii) specific fuel consumption in kg/kWh;

(iv) BMEP;

(v) volumetric efficiency;

(vi) brake thermal efficiency;

(vii) air to fuel ratio; and

(viii) equivalence ratio.

# Question 14 — Supercharger power and intercooled density boost

- Source: [Problem set Q12](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%205-6.pdf#page=1), PDF page 1.

An engine with an air mass flow rate of 0.1 kg/s has an inlet manifold pressure boost of 1.3 (absolute pressure ratio) provided by a supercharger. If flying at an altitude of 2000 m:

(i) determine the power required to drive the supercharger assuming a mechanical efficiency of 90% and isentropic efficiency of 80%;

(ii) if an intercooler reduces the supercharger temperature increase by 50%, determine the percentage increase in charge air density relative to atmospheric.

# Question 08 — Fuel-air Otto efficiency and mean effective pressure

- Source: [Problem set Q6A](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20-%20Week%203-4.pdf#page=1), PDF page 1.

A 6-cylinder SI aircraft engine operates at SSL on the fuel-air Otto cycle. Its total swept volume is 3.0 Litres, and the compression ratio is 9.33. Assume the fuel is gaseous isooctane and the A/F ratio is stoichiometric. The effective constant cycle properties are:

| Mixture | $c_v$ (kJ/(kg K)) | $\gamma$ |
|---|---:|---:|
| Reactants | 0.8392 | 1.327 |
| Products | 1.143 | 1.259 |

Assume no residual gases or combustion losses; fresh reactants occupy the full bottom-dead-centre volume at SSL pressure and temperature.

Calculate:

(i) the LHV (kJ/kg) of the fuel;

(ii) the fuel-air cycle thermal efficiency; and

(iii) the cycle MEP.

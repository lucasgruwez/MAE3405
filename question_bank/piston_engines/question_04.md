# Question 04 — Mars atmospheric mixture

- Source: [Problem set Q1](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%201-2.pdf#page=1), PDF page 1.

For the surface-level atmosphere of Mars, consider only CO₂, N₂, Ar, O₂, CO and H₂O, with their volume fractions normalised to unity. For Ar, $c_p=0.523$ kJ/(kg K) and molecular weight is 39.95 kg/kmol. Calculate:

(i) the average atmospheric molecular weight, noting that concentrations are volume fractions;

(ii) the gas constant $R$ in kJ/(kg K); and

(iii) the ratio of specific heats $\gamma$.

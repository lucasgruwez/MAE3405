# Question 15 — Net brake-power gain from mechanical supercharging

- Source: [Problem set Q13](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%205-6.pdf#page=1), PDF page 1.

An in-line 6-cylinder water cooled, 4-stroke aircraft engine of 75 mm bore and 100 mm stroke has a brake power output of 110 kW at 2800 RPM. The volumetric efficiency at this operating condition referred to SSL is 80%. The engine is now fitted with a mechanically driven supercharger with an isentropic efficiency of 70% and pressure ratio of 1.6. The supercharged version has a volumetric efficiency of 100% referred to supercharger delivery pressure and temperature. There is no intercooler.

Assume the indicated power developed per unit volume flow rate of induced air at ambient conditions is the same for normal aspiration and supercharging, and that the addition of the fuel can be ignored. Here indicated power means net indicated power. Calculate the net increase in brake power resulting from the supercharger. Take the mechanical efficiency of the engine at 80% in both cases and the mechanical efficiency of the supercharger drive as 95%.

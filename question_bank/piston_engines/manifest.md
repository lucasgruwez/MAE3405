# Piston-engine question-bank manifest

15 questions and 15 paired answers: examination Q1 from 2013, 2014 and 2015 first, followed by problem set Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5, Q6A, Q7, Q8, Q9, Q10, Q11, Q12 and Q13. Every source question retains all its subparts in one question/answer pair. No Q3, Q3D or Q14+ is included.

Original examination wording/tables/marks were transcribed and visually compared with the existing page PNGs; no full-page image is embedded. The 2013 molecular-weight unit typo is retained in its question and flagged in its answer. The official 2015 molecular-weight erratum is applied. Question files contain no Provided data sections, reference lookup directions, solution hints, printed answers or adaptation metadata. Students find reference data and relations unaided from the aide-mémoire and their personal cheatsheet; citations, guidance and provenance are kept in the answers and this manifest. Only necessary exercise-specific givens and physical model conditions remain in the question text before the tasks. The **2013 Avgas LHV 45.0 MJ/kg** is retained as a historical override to current Table 2; original exam tables and special reactant/product properties are untouched. Answers explicitly identify retained historical atmosphere/integer-mass rounding rather than reproducing redundant tables.

## Provenance and unresolved source limitations

- Problem-set PDF page numbers above are **physical PDF pages**. Week 3–4 PDF pages 1, 2 and 3 are printed question-bank pages 3, 4 and 5; Week 5–6 PDF page 1 is printed page 6.
- The aide-mémoire now supplies the previously missing gas/fuel/atmosphere/Mars and formation data; those are no longer labelled unavailable. Local 04 uses Table 8, truncating to six explicitly selected species and normalising; Table 1 omits Ar, so the original question's Ar data remain. The original O-360 engine-specification table is still unavailable, including in the aide-mémoire: local 10 retains a declared replacement operating dataset, and exact recovery of that variant remains blocked.
- Formation enthalpies for methanol and gaseous C₈H₁₈ are referenced to aide-mémoire Table 12, visually checked against Topic 3 p.32. Q6A's effective cycle properties remain from p.39, not generic room-temperature gas properties. The source Q9 answer retains the phase-specific liquid-isooctane LHV quoted on p.36 as a lookup, not a question given; Table 11's n-octane dataset is not silently substituted. Historical integer masses remain explicit hand-calculation conventions.
- No Cohen chart is provided in the aide-mémoire; none is required by these 15 pairs. Necessary physical model conditions, special cycle properties and the replacement engine dataset remain in the question prose/tables, not in a separate data section.
- Fuel-air solutions keep distinct reactant/product heat capacities and the 298 K reference-energy terms. Fuel heating-value efficiency is distinguished from efficiency relative to heat actually released. The unit's LHV-as-heat model is explicitly identified rather than mixed with an exact chemical internal-energy convention.
- Naturally aspirated examination solutions explicitly use the Topic 4 ambient-air volumetric-efficiency convention and include fuel mass in work output. The 2014 question explicitly fills swept volume with mixture, requiring mixture density and a mass split. The 2015 octane versus empirical-Avgas chemistry ambiguity is discussed rather than silently assumed away.
- Q2's published power/efficiency does not follow a consistent interpretation of its stated combined flow. Q7's displayed composition and Q13's declared net-power conventions also give results that differ from the printed answers. The answer files explain these discrepancies.
- Q10 plots are replaced by the 2000 RPM point. Q11 uses the 2000 m point and a scalar zero-excess-power ceiling with aide-mémoire density interpolation; the original approximation treating test power as SSL power is made explicit.

## Re-run checks

From the project root:

```sh
python3 question_bank/piston_engines/verify.py
```

The standard-library script recomputes and regression-checks final numerical values for all 15 pairs, asserts mass/energy and actuator-disc balances, checks that all 15 question/answer pairs exist, resolves all local Markdown links, checks display delimiters and forbidden boxed equations, and verifies examination marks, year-specific fuel data and molecular-weight erratum handling. It also rejects Provided data headings, renamed question sections, aide/lecture lookup directions, solution/adaptation metadata and duplicated reference tables; checks the retained engine/cycle/dynamometer givens; and verifies updated Mars properties. Aide-mémoire page links are checked only in this manifest, never required in question files. It does not claim that the missing engine specification table has been recovered or that published answers are authoritative.

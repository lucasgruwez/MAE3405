# Question 13 — Altitude power and an equivalent-airspeed ceiling

- Source: [Problem set Q11](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20-%20Week%203-4.pdf#page=3), PDF page 3.

The engine in Question 12 operates at 2700 RPM with a measured net brake load of 506 N on a 0.381 m torque arm. Treat the corresponding measured power as its SSL reference power $P_{b0}$. Its altitude performance follows the empirical law

$$P_b=P_{b0}\left(1.132\frac{\rho}{\rho_0}-0.132\right),$$

where $\rho$ is ambient air density and $\rho_0$ is SSL air density. The engine speed remains 2700 RPM.

(i) Determine brake power at **2000 m**.

(ii) Determine the maximum level-flight ceiling of an aircraft using this engine, at an equivalent airspeed of 150 knots and ceiling drag force 350 N. Assume constant propulsive efficiency 85%.

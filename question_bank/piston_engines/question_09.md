# Question 09 — Infer fuel and air flow from rich exhaust composition

- Source: [Problem set Q7](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20-%20Week%203-4.pdf#page=2), PDF page 2.

Measurement of the exhaust from a piston engine operating on a hydrocarbon fuel reveals the following gas concentrations (volume basis): CO₂ 9.14%, CO 5.1%, H₂O 13.7% and H₂ 2.31%. Nitrogen makes up the balance.

The mass flow rate of the exhaust is 0.1 kg/s. Determine:

(i) the mass flow rate (kg/s) of each exhaust species;

(ii) the fuel flow rate and air to fuel ratio; and

(iii) a possible fuel molecule in the form CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ (find $n$).

# Question 06 — Air-standard Otto cycle from temperature limits

- Source: [Problem set Q4](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20-%20Week%203-4.pdf#page=1), PDF page 1.

In an air standard Otto cycle the maximum and minimum temperatures are 1400°C and 15°C. The heat supplied to the cycle is 800 kJ/kg.

(i) Calculate the compression ratio and cycle thermal efficiency.

(ii) Calculate also the ratio of maximum to minimum pressures in the cycle assuming SSL as minimum cycle pressure.

(iii) What is the maximum possible thermal efficiency that could be obtained from the thermodynamic states that make up the cycle?

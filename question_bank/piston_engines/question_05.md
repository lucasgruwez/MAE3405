# Question 05 — Steady-flow energy balance and thermal efficiency

- Source: [Problem set Q2](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%201-2.pdf#page=1), PDF page 1.

An aircraft engine has a constant **combined air-plus-fuel mass flow rate of 0.2 kg/s**. It loses 15 kW of heat from its surfaces by radiation and convection. Fuel flow equals 1/15 of the inlet **air** mass flow, and fuel provides 45 MJ/kg.

Inlet air is at standard sea-level conditions. Exhaust is 65% N₂, 15% CO₂ and 20% water vapour by mass at 900°C. The inlet pipe diameter is 0.125 m and the exhaust pipe diameter is 0.15 m. Exhaust static pressure equals SSL atmospheric pressure, and fuel enters at the 25°C reference temperature with negligible kinetic energy. Assume steady operation and negligible potential-energy change. Determine:

(i) the shaft power output; and

(ii) the engine thermal efficiency based on fuel LHV.

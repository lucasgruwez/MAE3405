# Question 09 — Intercooled turbocharged engine and Froude thrust

Source: [Topics 7–8 problem set, Question 17*, printed p. 9 / PDF p. 1](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%207-8.pdf#page=1).

**Question 17*.** An intercooled turbocharged four-cylinder, four-stroke SI aircraft piston engine fuelled by avgas drives a 1.8m diameter twin-blade propeller. The turbocharger provides a pressure ratio of 1.3 at an isentropic efficiency of 85%. It is between the intake and fuel-injection system. The intercooler reduces the turbocharger temperature rise by 75%.

| Quantity | Value | Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Compression ratio | 7.0 | Combustion efficiency | 95% |
| Bore | 0.1m | Mechanical efficiency | 90% |
| Stroke | 0.12m | Volumetric efficiency | 100% |
| RPM | 2500 | Air to fuel ratio | 14.5 |
| $c_{pR}$ | 1.055kJ/(kg K) | $c_{pP}$ | 1.486kJ/(kg K) |
| $c_{vR}$ | 0.768kJ/(kg K) | $c_{vP}$ | 1.199kJ/(kg K) |

There is no intercooler pressure drop. Volumetric efficiency refers to air induction at intercooler outlet conditions. The turbocharger is exhaust driven, with negligible exhaust back-pressure and pumping penalties.

Assuming the fuel–air Otto cycle, calculate static thrust available at SSL using Froude theory.

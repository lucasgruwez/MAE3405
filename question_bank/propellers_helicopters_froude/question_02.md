# Question 02 — Main-rotor torque and tail-rotor power

Source: [2014 examination, Question 2, p. 2](../../past_exams/MAE3405%202014%20S2.PDF#page=2).

**2.** A helicopter with a mass of 2 tonnes hovers at an altitude of 1000m. The main rotor with diameter 10m has a blade tip Mach number of Ma=0.5 for this flight condition. The tail rotor with diameter 1.5m is located a distance of 6m from the main rotor shaft. Estimate the power required by the tail rotor to resist the torque generated by main rotor. List any assumptions. **[10 Marks]**

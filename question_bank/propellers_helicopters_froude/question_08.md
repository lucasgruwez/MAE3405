# Question 08 — Static propeller thrust and induced velocity

Source: [Topics 7–8 problem set, Question 16*, printed p. 9 / PDF p. 1](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%207-8.pdf#page=1).

**Question 16*.** A three-bladed propeller is statically tested at SSL, with zero advance speed, at 2700RPM. Its blade length from centre to tip is $R=1.0\ \mathrm m$, and the blade hub radius is 0.05m. Chord varies as
\[
c(r)=0.1R\left(1.1-\frac rR\right),
\]
and angle of attack varies with radius as
\[
\alpha(r)=12-4\frac rR\quad\text{degrees}.
\]
The section lift-curve slope is $a=0.15\ \mathrm{degree^{-1}}$, with zero lift at $\alpha=0$.

(i) Determine thrust produced.

(ii) Estimate induced velocity.

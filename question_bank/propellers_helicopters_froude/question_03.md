# Question 03 — Mi-6 payload and hover ceiling

Source: [2015 examination, Question 2, p. 2](../../past_exams/MAE3405%202015%20S2.PDF#page=2).

**2.** The Russian Mil Mi-6 heavy lift helicopter is powered by two turboshaft engines with SSL rated power 4050kW each. Without a payload, its take-off mass is 28,000kg. The 5 bladed main rotor has a diameter 35m. If an amount equal to 20% of the SSL rated engine power is required to drive the tail rotor and aircraft accessories at any power setting, determine: (a) the maximum SSL level payload that can just be lifted from the ground and (b) the maximum altitude the helicopter can hover without a payload. **[5+5=10 Marks]**

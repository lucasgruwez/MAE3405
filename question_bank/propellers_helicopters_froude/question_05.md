Source: [Topics 5–6 problem set, Question 14*, printed p. 7 / PDF p. 2](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%205-6.pdf#page=2).

**Question 14*.** A 2m diameter propeller is tested on the four-cylinder SI engine below, running on avgas at an altitude of 2000m.

| Quantity | Value | Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Compression ratio | 7.5 | Combustion efficiency | 99% |
| Bore | 0.1m | Mechanical efficiency | 85% |
| Stroke | 0.125m | Volumetric efficiency | 80% |
| RPM | 2700 | Air to fuel ratio | 14.5 |
| $c_{pR}$ | 1.055kJ/(kg K) | $c_{pP}$ | 1.486kJ/(kg K) |
| $c_{vR}$ | 0.768kJ/(kg K) | $c_{vP}$ | 1.199kJ/(kg K) |

The engine operates on a four-stroke fuel–air Otto cycle. Volumetric efficiency refers to air induction at ambient conditions.

Determine the static thrust developed.

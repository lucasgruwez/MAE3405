# Question 07 — Quadcopter endurance in a headwind

Source: [Topics 5–6 problem set, Question 16D, printed p. 8 / PDF p. 3](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%205-6.pdf#page=3).

**Question 16D.** A quadcopter drone hovers at 200ft, maintaining a steady position in a constant horizontal wind of 36km/h. Each of its four rotors has diameter 4in. Gross mass is 1.5kg and drag coefficient is 0.75. Its brushless DC motors have KV rating 1200RPM/V and operate at one sixth of maximum torque, with efficiency 80%. The total battery capacity is 1.3Ah at 22.2V.

The drag coefficient uses the sum of the four rotor disk areas as its reference area. The resultant relative airspeed through the disks is 16.086m/s. All stored battery energy is usable at constant voltage; 80% is the combined electrical-to-shaft efficiency, with no other electrical loads. The motors follow a linear torque–speed characteristic at 22.2V, and maximum torque means stall torque. Use $1\ \mathrm{ft}=0.3048\ \mathrm m$ and $1\ \mathrm{in}=0.0254\ \mathrm m$.

Calculate:

(i) how long the drone can maintain position;

(ii) rotor RPM.

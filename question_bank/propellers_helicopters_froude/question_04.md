# Question 04 — Three-phase propeller-drive motor

Source: [Topics 1–2 problem set, Question 3D, p. 2](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%201-2.pdf#page=2).

**Question 3D.** A 5-horsepower electric motor for a light aircraft operates on an alternating-current supply generated from the aircraft's batteries using a three-phase inverter. The voltage provided to the motor is 440V AC RMS. Under no-load conditions it runs at 1195RPM and draws 1.2A RMS at a power factor of 0.30 lagging. Connected to the propeller at full throttle, it draws 6.8A at a power factor of 0.78 lagging and runs at 1150RPM.

The supply is balanced; 440V is line-to-line RMS voltage and the currents are line currents. The motor delivers 5hp of shaft power at the specified full-load condition; $1\ \mathrm{hp}=746\ \mathrm W$.

Find the motor's input power, power loss and efficiency at no load and full load. Calculate its speed regulation.

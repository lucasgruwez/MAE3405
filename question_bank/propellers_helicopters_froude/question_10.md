# Question 10 — Mi-6 problem-set payload and ceiling

Source: [Topics 7–8 problem set, Question 17C*, printed p. 10 / PDF p. 2](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%207-8.pdf#page=2).

**Question 17C*.** The Russian Mil Mi-6 heavy lift helicopter is powered by two turboshaft engines with SSL rate power of 4050 kW each. Without a payload, the take-off mass is 28,000 kg. The 5-bladed main rotor has a diameter of 35 m. An amount equal to 20% of the SSL rated engine power is required to drive the tail rotor and aircraft accessories at any power setting. Total available engine power varies in proportion to ambient air density; the tail-rotor/accessory allowance remains fixed at 20% of SSL rated power.

Determine:

(i) The maximum SSL level payload that can just be lifted from the ground

(ii) The maximum altitude the helicopter can hover without a payload.

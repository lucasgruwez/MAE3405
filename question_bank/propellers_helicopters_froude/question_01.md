# Question 01 — Helicopter blade lift and rotor power

Source: [2013 examination, Question 2, p. 2](../../past_exams/MAE3405%202013%20S2.PDF#page=2).

**2.** A helicopter blade has a chord of 250mm and its tip is 3.5m from its centre of rotation. The blade is fixed a distance of 0.4m from the centre of rotation. A shaft from the engine drives the blade. The angle of attack of the blade is such that the lift coefficient is constant at Cₗ=1.0 along its length. The advance speed can be assumed to be zero. (a) If the blade mass is 20kg, at what RPM will the drive shaft experience zero vertical force at SSL? (b) If there are 4 blades attached to the shaft, estimate the power required to drive the rotor. **[10+5=15 Marks]**

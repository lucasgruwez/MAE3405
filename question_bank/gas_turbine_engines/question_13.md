# Question 13 — Fuelled ramjet with an oblique/normal-shock intake

Source: [Topics 9–10 problem set](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%209-10.pdf), Q28B, PDF p. 4 (printed p. 14 of 16).

## Question

A ram jet operates on fuel which can be approximated by C10H22. The combustor fuel/air ratio is 0.03. The inlet conditions consist of a single oblique shock at a wave angle of 30° terminated by a normal shock, followed by subsonic flow through the entry nozzle into the combustor. The exit conditions are a fully expanded convergent-divergent propelling nozzle. The efficiency of the combustor and nozzles is 95%, and combustor pressure loss is 5% of the ram pressure. For specific heats, assume the fuel is similar to Jet A1. Use a constant-property combustion model with hot-product cp = 1.148 kJ/(kg K) and γ = 1.333. The 95% nozzle efficiency applies to both the subsonic intake diffuser and the propelling nozzle, and the combustion pressure loss is relative to recovered inlet total pressure.

Determine the specific thrust for a cruise speed of Mach 2.5 at an altitude of 20,000 m.

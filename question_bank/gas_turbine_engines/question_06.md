| Isentropic Efficiencies | | Turbine Cp | 1.148 kJ/kg.K |
| Compressor | 87% | Turbine γ | 1.333 |
| Turbine | 87% | Exhaust Gas Constant R | 0.287 kJ/kg.K |
| Intake | 95% | Engine Mass Flow | 15 kg/s |

| Mechanical Efficiency | 99% | Altitude | 7000 m |

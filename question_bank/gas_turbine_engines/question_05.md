# Question 05 — Ideal turbojet at two operating points

Source: [Topics 9–10 problem set](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%209-10.pdf), Q20(i–ii), PDF p. 1 (printed p. 11 of 16).

## Question

A single spool turbojet with a fixed overall pressure ratio of 20 and maximum cycle temperature of 1400 K operates with an ideal compressor, turbine and nozzles. Assume that the specific heats are the same for cold and hot sides. Assume fuel mass flow is negligible with respect to air mass flow. Use Jet A1 fuel, an ideal intake and combustor with no combustion pressure or shaft losses, and an ideal converging propelling nozzle.

(i) At Mach 0.8 and altitude 10,000 m, calculate the specific thrust and TSFC.

(ii) At Mach 0.4 and altitude 5000 m, calculate the specific thrust and TSFC.

For each operating point, also calculate the specific thrust and TSFC with an ideal fully expanded converging-diverging propelling nozzle.

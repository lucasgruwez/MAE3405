# Question 12 — Ramjet with a fully expanding nozzle

Source: [Topics 9–10 problem set](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%209-10.pdf), Q27, PDF p. 4 (printed p. 14 of 16), referring to Q26 on the same page.

## Question

A ramjet operates at an altitude of 20,000 m with a maximum combustor total temperature of 2200 K. Use Jet A1 fuel, 100% combustion efficiency, a constant-property combustion model and a combustion pressure drop of 1% of recovered inlet total pressure. The hot products have cp = 1.148 kJ/(kg K) and γ = 1.333. Above Mach 1 the intake uses a normal shock in the inlet plane followed by a subsonic diffuser of isentropic efficiency 95%.

The propelling nozzle is converging with 95% isentropic efficiency at Mach 1, and is an isentropic converging-diverging nozzle fully expanding to atmospheric pressure above Mach 1. The nozzle inlet total pressures at the endpoints are:

| Flight Mach | Nozzle inlet total pressure (after combustion loss) |
|---|---|
| 1 | 9.96378 kPa |
| 5 | 175.95584 kPa |

Calculate the specific thrust and TSFC at Mach 1.75.

Calculate the nozzle exit-to-minimum-area ratios at Mach 1 and Mach 5. Discuss whether acceleration from Mach 1 to Mach 5 is practical with fixed or variable nozzle geometry.

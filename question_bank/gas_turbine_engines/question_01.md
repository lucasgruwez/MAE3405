## Question

3. A new twin spool, aft-fan engine is being designed and you are tasked with calculating the cruise design point thrust (Ma=0.8 at 10,000m). The layout of this engine is based on a gas generator spool and a free (or power) spool arrangement. For the gas generator spool, the turbine stage drives the compressor stage, while for the free spool, which is located directly after the gas generator, the turbine directly drives the aft-fan. Similar to a turbofan engine, the fan on this engine is housed in a duct which passes the cold flow to a nozzle. Similar to a turboprop, the hot flow is passed to a nozzle with a fixed nozzle pressure ratio (PNPR), see below. Being an aft-fan engine, there are separate inlet nozzles for the engine core and the fan. [30 Marks]

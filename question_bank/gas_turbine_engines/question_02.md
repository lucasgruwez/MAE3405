# Question 02 — Aft-fan ramjet with two-shock intake

Source: [2014 examination](../../past_exams/MAE3405%202014%20S2.PDF), Q3, p. 2; data sheets pp. 3–7; [official erratum](../../past_exams/MAE3405%202014%20S2%20Err.PDF), p. 1. [Original page](../assets/exam_2014_questions.png).

## Question

3. You are required to calculate the thrust for the cruise condition of a new engine design. Known as an aft-fan-ramjet, once at its cruise condition (Ma=1.7, Alt=20,000m) the engine operates as follows. The intake is a supersonic inlet comprised of one oblique shock terminated by a single normal shock. The shock wave angle of the oblique shock is 50°. A subsonic section directly follows the supersonic intake. After this, similar to a turbofan engine the flow separates into two streams. The hot stream passes directly to a combustor, then on to a turbine (this turbine directly drives the aft-fan), after which it flows to a converging nozzle. The cold stream passes directly to the aft-fan, after which it flows to a converging nozzle. The hot and cold nozzles are separate. Similar to a turbofan engine, the aft-fan is operated at a fixed pressure ratio (FPR) and by-pass for the cruise condition. Operational data is listed below. Assume fuel mass flow is small relative to engine mass flow. [35 Marks]

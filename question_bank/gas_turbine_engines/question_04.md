# Question 04 — Gas-turbine continuity, thrust and TSFC

Source: [Topics 1–2 problem set](../../problem_sets/MAE3405%20Problem%20Set%20Questions%20Week%201-2.pdf), Q3(a–c), PDF p. 2 (printed p. 2 of 16).

## Question

A small gas turbine engine has the following flight conditions. You may assume the exhaust pressure to be atmospheric.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Altitude | 6500 m |
| Flight speed Mach number | 0.398 |
| Intake diameter | 0.300 m |
| Exhaust diameter | 0.285 m |
| Exhaust speed | 950 kph |
| Exhaust gas temperature | 250°C |
| Exhaust gas constant | 250 J/kg.K |
| Fuel density | 850 kg/m³ |

(a) Determine the ratio of fuel mass flow to air mass flow for the engine.

(b) Calculate the net thrust developed by the engine and the thrust specific fuel consumption (in kg/N.h).

(c) What percentage of the gross thrust is used to overcome ram drag?
